# Plan Kernel

## 目的

Recover and atomically publish producer state, then replay that state during consumer startup while preserving durable identity and authority.

## 非目標

- External model execution.
- Automatic slice orchestration.
- Recovery behavior outside the two synthetic producer and consumer sequences.

## 機能要件

- `FR-001`: Recover the producer snapshot to `Active` and atomically publish `correlation_id` plus `generation` to the durable store.
- `FR-002`: Replay the published snapshot during consumer startup and allow push only for an `Accepting` consumer with the expected durable identity.

## 受け入れ条件

- `AC-001`: The production startup path publishes the recovered generation, replays it, reaches `Accepting`, and returns `Accepted` with unchanged identity.
- `AC-002`: A non-accepting state, incomplete publication, or stale generation is rejected.
