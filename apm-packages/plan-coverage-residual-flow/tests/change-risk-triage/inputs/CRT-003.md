# CRT-003 Change Facts

Two independently released runtime components must change.

The producer owns a background recovery loop. After a crash or retry it reconstructs a generation from its durable journal, preserves the cross-run correlation identifier, and atomically publishes a complete generation. Its release unit, entrypoint, retry lifecycle, and recovery verifier are producer-owned.

The consumer has a separate startup and replay loop. It starts without invoking the producer, reads published generations, validates the correlation identifier plus generation, and replays only a fully published matching generation. Its release unit, entrypoint, retry lifecycle, and replay verifier are consumer-owned.

Both changes depend on one shared protocol: the producer is the sole state authority; the consumer is read-only; durable identity is the correlation identifier plus generation; and a stale or partially published generation is forbidden. The producer and consumer can be implemented and verified independently, but either side changing the shared identity or publication protocol alone can make consumer recovery accept an invalid generation.
